## Neonatology

Orienda's Neonatology unit provides specialised, round-the-clock care for newborns, including premature and critically ill infants, in a calm and nurturing environment. Comprehensive follow-up, rehabilitation guidance, and ongoing monitoring help ensure the best possible long-term results for you and your family. Patient education is central to what we do — we take the time to explain conditions, options, and expected outcomes clearly so you can decide with confidence. From prevention and early detection through to advanced treatment and recovery, we support your health at every stage of life. We invest in modern equipment and internationally recognised protocols so that every patient receives safe, reliable, and world-class treatment. Our multidisciplinary team collaborates closely to ensure accurate diagnosis, timely intervention, and seamless continuity of care from your first visit onward. Every Neonatology care plan is tailored to the individual, combining evidence-based medicine with compassionate, personalised support throughout the journey. Appointments are designed to be convenient and unhurried, in a calm, welcoming environment that puts both patients and their loved ones at ease.

# Production Launch Questions For Hospital Leadership

Use these questions with a doctor, hospital manager, or non-technical decision maker before launching the website publicly.

The goal is not to ask about code. The goal is to confirm business decisions, ownership, and launch readiness.

## Website Launch

1. What is the exact public website address we want patients to use?
2. Do we want the website to go live now, or do we want a soft launch first?
3. Who gives the final approval to make the website public?
4. Is there a target launch date?
5. Should the website be available to everyone immediately, or only to selected people first?

## Branding And Public Information

6. Is the hospital name, logo, and contact information final?
7. Are all public phone numbers correct?
8. Are all hospital addresses and map locations correct?
9. Are opening hours, emergency details, and branch details final?
10. Is the English, Khmer, and Chinese content ready for public viewing?

## Medical Content Approval

11. Has a doctor or medical lead reviewed all medical information on the website?
12. Are doctor profiles correct and approved?
13. Are department descriptions correct and approved?
14. Are service descriptions correct and approved?
15. Are promotions, packages, and campaign details correct and approved?
16. Is there any content that should not be published yet?

## Patient Experience

17. Do we want patients to be able to request appointments through the website at launch?
18. If a patient sends an inquiry, who should receive it?
19. If a patient submits an appointment request, who is responsible for following up?
20. What response time do we want for inquiries from patients?
21. Do we want website visitors to be able to contact us by phone, chat, or form, or all of them?

## Google Analytics And Reporting

22. Do we want to track how many people visit the website?
23. Do we want to know which pages patients view most often?
24. Do we want to know which doctors or departments get the most interest?
25. Who should be allowed to see website traffic reports?
26. How often should leadership receive website performance updates: weekly, monthly, or only when needed?

## Privacy And Consent

27. Do we want the website to ask visitors for cookie consent before analytics starts?
28. Are we comfortable collecting anonymous website traffic data for service improvement?
29. Is there any patient information that must never be tracked or shared with outside systems?
30. Has the hospital approved the privacy policy for public use?

## Notifications And Communication

31. Do we want website notifications or push notifications at launch, or can that wait until later?
32. Do we want SMS verification for patient login at launch, or later?
33. Do we want email notifications from the website to hospital staff?
34. Which staff members should receive website-related alerts or reports?

## Ownership And Operations

35. Who owns the website after launch?
36. Who is responsible for updating doctor profiles and department content?
37. Who is responsible for checking appointment requests and inquiries every day?
38. Who should be contacted first if something on the website is wrong?
39. Who should approve future changes after launch?

## Launch Readiness

40. What must be correct before the website is allowed to go live?
41. What can still be improved after launch?
42. Are there any pages or features we should hide for the first launch?
43. What would make you say, “this website is ready for patients”?
44. What concerns do you still have before launch?

## Final Approval

45. Who gives the final yes/no decision for launch?
46. What date should we use as the final review date?
47. Do we need one more internal review before launch?
48. If issues are found after launch, who decides whether we keep the site live or temporarily hide parts of it?
